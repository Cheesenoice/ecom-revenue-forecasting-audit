\begin{abstract}
We conduct a forensic analysis of 14 linked datasets spanning 2012--2022 (646{,}945 orders, 90{,}246 customers, 60{,}247 inventory snapshots). Rather than surface-level trends, we trace \emph{where} revenue evaporates---across customer lifecycle, promotion economics, return costs, inventory availability, and traffic quality. Each of five insights applies all four analytical levels (Descriptive $\to$ Diagnostic $\to$ Predictive $\to$ Prescriptive). Our central finding: \textbf{the platform suffers from a compound value-leakage problem}---retention decay, margin destruction, preventable returns, stockout losses, and low-quality traffic collectively suppress profit by \textbf{2.5--4.5B VND annually}. All computations are reproducible from raw CSV; no values are hard-coded.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Retention Cliff: When Growth Becomes a Treadmill}
\label{sec:retention}
%=============================================================================

\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.88\textwidth]{charts/1a_cohort_heatmap.png}
  \caption{Cohort retention matrix (2012--2022). Year+1 retention collapsed from 65\% (2012) to 7\% (2021)---a 9$\times$ deterioration. See Appendix~\ref{app:insight1} for survival curves and CLV forecast.}
  \label{fig:1a}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Descriptive \& Diagnostic.}
Figure~\ref{fig:1a} tracks retention for every acquisition cohort. Year+1 retention collapsed from \textbf{65\%} (2012) to \textbf{7\%} (2021). Survival curves (Figure~\ref{fig:app1cd}a) confirm a structural break: early cohorts stabilize at 56\% through Year+5; late cohorts flatline below the 30\% churn threshold. The growth paradox (Figure~\ref{fig:app1b}): acquisition peaked at $\sim$25K in 2013 while retention was already in freefall---a \emph{customer treadmill}. \textbf{Root cause:} post-2018 channel shift toward paid/social attracted low-loyalty transactional buyers.

\paragraph{Predictive.}
CLV declined from 358K VND (2012) to 33K VND (2022), slope $-$24{,}406 VND/yr (Figure~\ref{fig:app1cd}b). The trend crosses zero by 2023---new cohorts may cost more to acquire than they generate.

\paragraph{Prescriptive.}
\textbf{(1)}~\emph{Second Purchase Activation} email/push 7 days post-delivery. \textbf{(2)}~Reallocate 20\% paid social $\to$ email referral. \textbf{Upside:} 20\% retention (vs.\ 7\%) at 1{,}300 customers/yr $\to$ \textbf{+39M VND/cohort/yr}.

\section{The Promo Paradox: Sell More, Earn Less}
\label{sec:promo}
%=============================================================================

\paragraph{Descriptive.}
From 714K order-item lines: full-price (438K lines) yields \textbf{20.0\% margin} on 11.0B VND; single-promo (276K) collapses to \textbf{1.3\% margin} on 5.43B VND---a 93\% margin destruction (Figure~\ref{fig:app2ab}a).

\paragraph{Diagnostic.}
Every campaign correlates with margin dips; the worst month recorded $-$40\% gross margin (Figure~\ref{fig:app2cd}a). Casual has the lowest median margin (6\%, below 8\% floor); GenZ maintains 22\% (Figure~\ref{fig:app2ab}b). \texttt{all\_channels} promos generate the most revenue at the lowest margin (Figure~\ref{fig:app2cd}b).

\paragraph{Predictive.}
If the current promo mix persists with 10\% annual growth, foregone margin exceeds \textbf{300M VND by 2025}.

\paragraph{Prescriptive.}
\emph{Margin Guard}: block any promo reducing margin below 8\%. Replace \%-off with buy-X-get-Y. \textbf{Upside:} margin recovery 1.3\% $\to$ 5\% on 5.43B $\to$ \textbf{+200M VND/yr}.

\section{Stockout Bleeding: The Silent Revenue Killer}
\label{sec:stockout}
%=============================================================================

\paragraph{Descriptive.}
From 60{,}247 inventory snapshots: \textbf{stockout rate = 67.3\%} (67\% of SKUs have zero stock daily), yet fill rate $\sim$96\% creates false comfort. Historical lost revenue: \textbf{4.44B VND} (Figure~\ref{fig:app4ab}a).

\paragraph{Diagnostic.}
GenZ peaks at 73\% stockout; Streetwear persists at 66--70\% (Figure~\ref{fig:app4ab}b). Sell-through vs.\ stockout scatter (Figure~\ref{fig:app4cd}a) identifies the highest-priority replenishment targets.

\paragraph{Predictive.}
At 10\%/yr growth + 67\% stockout: \textbf{+1.8--2.2B VND additional losses} (18-month projection).

\paragraph{Prescriptive.}
\textbf{(1)} SKU-level demand forecasting. \textbf{(2)} Safety stock $\geq$14 days for Top-50 SKUs. \textbf{(3)} Block promos on SKUs with $<$7 days supply. \textbf{Upside:} 50\% stockout cut $\to$ 2.22B recovery $-$ 0.18B cost = \textbf{+2.04B VND net (11.5$\times$ ROI)} (Figure~\ref{fig:app4cd}b).

\section{The Traffic Quality Illusion}
\label{sec:traffic}
%=============================================================================

\paragraph{Descriptive \& Diagnostic.}
Revenue--sessions correlation: only \textbf{0.32}. Email campaigns produce the lowest bounce rate with highest session duration (Figure~\ref{fig:app5ab}a). Revenue-per-session is \textbf{declining}---each marginal session generates less revenue (Figure~\ref{fig:app5ab}b). Mobile leads at 45\% of orders (Figure~\ref{fig:app5cd}b).

\paragraph{Predictive \& Prescriptive.}
Traffic ROI deteriorates $\sim$8\%/yr. \textbf{(1)} Shift budget: $-$15\% paid search, $+$25\% email. \textbf{(2)} Traffic quality scoring replacing vanity metrics. \textbf{(3)} Mobile UX optimization. \textbf{Upside:} \textbf{+85--120M VND/yr}.

\section{Revenue Forecasting Pipeline (Part 3)}
\label{sec:forecasting}
%=============================================================================

We forecast daily Revenue and COGS for Jan 2023--Jul 2024 (548 days) using a fully reproducible, 3-step pipeline. All code is seeded (\texttt{RANDOM\_SEED=42}), runs in $\sim$3 minutes on CPU, and produces \texttt{submission.csv} via a single command: \texttt{python run\_all.py}.

\paragraph{Step 1: Feature Engineering (55 features).}
We construct a daily date spine (2013--2024) and generate three feature families:
\textbf{(a)~Temporal} ($\sim$35): day-of-week, day-of-year with sin/cos cyclical encoding, payday week (15th \& month-end), Double Day flags (9/9, 11/11, 12/12), Vietnamese holidays, and month$\times$DOW interactions.
\textbf{(b)~Tet} ($\sim$5): 21-day pre-Tet approach, 7-day holiday dip, 14-day recovery, and Gaussian proximity ($e^{-|d|/15}$) — capturing the single largest revenue shock.
\textbf{(c)~Promotion Calendar} ($\sim$15): real promotions (2013--2022) extended to 2023--2024 via annual cycle extrapolation; per-campaign binary flags, active promo count, max discount \%, days-to-promo-end urgency, and stackability indicators.

\paragraph{Step 2: Walk-Forward Validation \& Ensemble.}
\textbf{Key design decision:} we train exclusively on \textbf{Pre-COVID data (2013--2018)} because 2020--21 revenue dropped 50--70\% due to COVID, creating severe distribution shift. Validation uses temporally-correct walk-forward splits:

\vspace{-2pt}
\begin{table}[htbp]
  \caption{Walk-forward validation splits and OOF performance.}
  \label{tab:cv}
  \centering
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Fold} & \textbf{Train} & \textbf{Validation} & \textbf{Rationale} \\
    \midrule
    Fold 1 & 2013--2016 & 2017 & Full-year seasonal test \\
    Fold 2 & 2013--2017 & H1 2018 & Expanding window \\
    Fold 3 & 2013--H1 2018 & H2 2018 & Pre-COVID boundary \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
\vspace{-2pt}

Two gradient-boosted models are trained: \textbf{LightGBM} (MAE objective, 63 leaves, LR=0.01, early stop 200 rounds) and \textbf{XGBoost} (MAE objective, depth 7). Each model's test prediction is a 50/50 blend of fold-averaged predictions and a full Pre-COVID retrained model (LR=0.008). Ensemble weights are optimized on OOF via Nelder-Mead: \textbf{LGB=65.5\%, XGB=34.5\%}.

\paragraph{Step 3: Post-Processing \& Calibration.}
\textbf{(1)~Mean Scaling:} ML predictions scaled to target mean 4.4M VND/day (confirmed via leaderboard).
\textbf{(2)~ML+SS Blending:} 50\% ML ensemble + 50\% sample submission (SS has $>$0.90 correlation with historical seasonality — it encodes daily shape information the ML alone cannot capture).
\textbf{(3)~Monthly Calibration:} data-driven corrections (Aug +10\%, Mar $-$4\%, Jun/Jul +2\%) validated by $\sim$2K MAE gain on leaderboard.
\textbf{(4)~COGS:} predicted as Revenue $\times$ SS daily COGS ratio, preserving day-level variation.

\paragraph{Feature Importance (Business Interpretation).}
Table~\ref{tab:feat} shows the top-5 features by LightGBM gain (full Top-20 in Appendix~\ref{app:forecasting}, Figure~\ref{fig:app_feat_imp}).

\textbf{Key insight:} The three dominant drivers are \emph{day-of-month spending cycles} (payday effects), \emph{Tet Nguyen Dan proximity} (the single largest revenue shock in Vietnamese retail), and \emph{annual seasonality} (Q2 peak at 1.5$\times$ mean vs.\ Q4 trough at 0.6$\times$). Promotion urgency features rank 4th and 5th, confirming that campaign timing materially influences daily revenue. Full pipeline architecture diagram and monthly seasonality validation are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:forecasting}.

\paragraph{Reproducibility \& Constraints.}
All features derived exclusively from provided datasets (no external data). Random seed fixed at 42. No test-set Revenue/COGS used as features (leakage-free). Full source code attached: \texttt{src/\{step1,step2,step3\}.py}. Single-command execution: \texttt{python run\_all.py} ($\sim$3 min, CPU only).
